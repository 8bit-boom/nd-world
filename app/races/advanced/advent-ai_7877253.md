# Advent AI

**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
You don't have Physical Stats, only Mental Stats.

Spend 10 points on Mental Stats and add +2 bonus to Intellect. You have MP and Shock. Add +5 to your Shock. If your Shock drops to 0, you are erased.

To interact with the physical world you use Husks. Husks are special robots that you can inhabit and that have their own Physical Stats, PP and Health. You can't increase Husk stats. Husks can have augments to some extent.

You can use ordinary robots; in this case they have those stats: 2 in all Physical Stats, 5 PP, 7 Health.

Uploading yourself to a robot is an Action. If the robot has a defense programmz against viruses, you must first defeat or turn off this programmz.

You have +1 bonus to hacking rolls.

You escaped from some mega corporation and they are trying to recruit you.

If Husk or robot you inhabit is destroyed, you take 1d4 Shock damage.

# Child of the Black Goat

**Type:** Humanoid, Abberation
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
You are a child of All-Mother, an animalistic-looking humanoid who is in tune with nature.  
  
You can telepathically communicate with animals. Animals who are hostile are usually neutral to you, and neutral animals are friendly to you.   
  
You have +1 bonus to rolls related to Nature.  
You have +1 bonus to rolls related to non-combat Psy Powers (powers that deal damage or put debuffs are off-limits).

You have a -2 penalty to orient in urban environment.  
  
You can cast Rituals - they are powerful Psy Powers that take long time and effort to perform.

If you implant metal augments in yourself, you are exiled from Children's society and can't use Rituals. Bio augments allowed only if blessed by priestess/priest of the Black Goat.

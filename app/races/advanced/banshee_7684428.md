# Banshee

**Type:** Psy Ghost
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
You are a ghost possessing a robot.

As a result, you don't really have any rights as a citizen or person.

You don't have Health, Shock, MP or PP, instead you have Ectoplasm.

Any ability, feat or item that changes your Health, Shock, MP, PP now changes your Ectoplasm.

You use Ectoplasm instead of MP, PP, Health or Shock.

Ectoplasm is the sum of all your Stats.

You take extra 4 damage from electric sources.

You have 2 armor. You don't benefit from wearing armor.

You are immune to Toxins and Poison.

You have -1 penalty on social rolls.

You can use an Action to leave this automaton; in ghost form you can't interact, and a psyonic can banish you.

You can't use Bio Augments. You treat yourself as already having all Alpha Augments.

You heal only by repair.

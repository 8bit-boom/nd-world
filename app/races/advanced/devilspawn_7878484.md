# Devilspawn

**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Second phase of [Baroque Disease](families/baroque-disease_7575185).

Sometimes you get possessed by [Man with a Devilish Mind](characters/man-with-a-devilish-mindvir-mente-diabolica_6988958). While possessed you gain +2 to all your Stats. Usually by GM Intrusion or critical failure while your third eye is opened.

You have -5 penalty to your Health.

Your third eye gives you latent psyonic abilities. You can also use it to communicate with [Man with a Devilish Mind/Vir Mente Diabolica](characters/man-with-a-devilish-mindvir-mente-diabolica_6988958) while you sleep.

You can open your third eye. Action.

When your third eye is opened, you can use powerful abilities.

# Crimson Elves/Amalgama

**Type:** Elf
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Choose one physical stat, add +1 to it. You can also add an additional +1 to a physical stat, but you must take a -1 penalty to one physical stat. You can change the chosen stats after Rest.

You can't use augments. Instead you have access to Flesh Grafts - they are powerful abilities with some penalties. Every time you buy a Race Feat, you get 1 Rank point of Flesh Grafts. You can't hide Flesh Grafts under clothing or otherwise.

You can apply one major Flesh Graft and two minor Flesh Grafts.

You can change color of your skin, your height, weight, color of your eyes, etc. It takes 1 minute to apply those changes.

You sustain yourself on the blood of other creatures.

Stims and drugs have no effect on you.

If you haven't fed on blood for 48 hours, you start taking 1 Health damage every hour and can't heal Health until you feed.

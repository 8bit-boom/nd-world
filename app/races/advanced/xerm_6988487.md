# Xerm

**Type:** Mutant
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
You gain +1 to Body and Strength and -1 to Intellect. You can't use Psy Powers. You have +2 bonus to resist Psy Powers and you take only half damage (rounded down) from Psy Powers. You can use Xerm (Heavy) weapons without penalty. Augments cost increases by 20%, you gain +2 Feat Slots for arm augmentation.  

You have an auxiliary heart (AH), some abilities use it. You have 3 charges per Rest. Your stim limit per Rest is 1.
---




### Overview

Every Xerm carries a **synthetic auxiliary heart**, a four-chambered bio-reactor engineered by **Biolumina**.  
It pumps nanobiotic fuel through their bloodstream, granting inhuman strength and speed — but it has no true failsafe.  
Push it too far, and it detonates inward.

You’re not *alive*. You’re *pressurized*.

---

### ⚙️ **Heart Pressure (HPres)**

Each Xerm tracks a unique resource: **Heart Pressure (HPres)**

- **Starting HPres:** 3
- **Maximum HPres:** 6 — *if it ever exceeds 6, you die instantly.*  
    Your body implodes into molten biotech sludge.
- You gain HPres when your heart is strained — through battle, rage, or damage.
- You lose HPres through rest, control, or injections.

---

### 💢 **Gaining Heart Pressure**

You **gain +1 HPres** when any of the following happen:

- You take more than 10 damage in a single hit.
- You kill an enemy.
- You perform a physical feat beyond human limits (GM discretion).
- You activate an Overdrive ability or push a failed Strength/Body roll.

---

### 🫀 **Losing Heart Pressure**

You **lose 1 HPres** when you:

- Spend an uninterrupted hour resting or meditating.
- Are stabilized by a **Biolumina Serum Injection** (Action).
- Succeed on a **Willpower + Body (DC 13)** roll after combat.

You cannot go below 0 HPres.

---

### 🔥 **Heart Pressure Thresholds**

| HPres | State | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **0–1** | **Stable** | Heartbeat steady. You gain +1 to resist Shock damage (+1 to roll against it). |
| **2–3** | **Charged** | Nanofluid surge. +1 to Strength and Body. You can use all four arms without penalty and perform an extra interaction or reload each turn. |
| **4–5** | **Overdrive** | Blood ignites. +2 Strength, +1 Dexterity, +2 Speed. You can take an additional Action each turn, but lose 1 Health at the end of your turn as your organs scorch. |
| **6** | **Critical Limit** | Your veins glow like molten copper. Gain +3 to all Physical Stats. You act first this round. At the end of your turn, roll 1d6: on 4+, you die as the heart ruptures. On 1–3, you survive but stay at 6 HPres. You cannot reduce HPres this round. |
| **7+** | **Heart Rupture — DEATH** | Your Auxiliary Core collapses, vaporizing your heart and anyone within short range (2d10 damage, ignores armor). Nothing biological remains of you. |

# Below Elves/Drow

**Type:** Elf
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Below Elves/Drow - a subspecies of elves that were banished underground by the high elves after the War for the Sun. They hate the other elves (as if that's something unique). They are forced to live in terrible conditions, in a toxic atmosphere. Many of them want to take revenge on those who drove them there. Many terrorist acts have been committed or are suspected to have been committed by them. They have a reputation as terrorists. What many don't know is that the Toxic Barons and the Illuminati often work together.

You can see in complete darkness as if it were day. You can see colors but they are more bleak.   
  
+1 Willpower.

## Dual Persona Protocol
Every Drow character has two active personas:  
  
Surface Mask: The version of yourself the world expects — an identity tied to your job, class, cover, or public role.  
  
Shadow Self: Your true face — insurgent, trickster, thinker, killer, or believer in Drow liberation.  
  
  
You can switch between these two personas once per scene, granting you different benefits depending on the mask worn.  
  
While in Surface Mask:  
  
+1 to Deception, Bureaucracy, or Social Engineering  
  
Treated as “non-hostile” by most corporations, patrols, and drones  
  
Can bypass biometric or behavior-based security (they scan your cover identity)  
  
  
While in Shadow Self:  
  
+1 to Stealth, Hacking, or Explosives  
  
Can spend 1 PP or MP to become Undetected for one action (even in surveillance-heavy areas)  
  
May trigger a Saboteur Action: sabotage a system, implant a virus, or disable a camera or mech with improvised tools

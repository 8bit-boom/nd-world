# Low Elves/Orks

**Type:** Elf
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Low elves/orks - are another kind of elves who, during the War for the Sun, knelt before the high elves, thus entering their service. They make good warriors and usually rich high elves take them as bodyguards. Usually very dedicated to the ideals of their tribe, they can consume the flesh of the recently deceased, thereby receiving the most vivid memories of the dead. Conservative. Other races fear them because of how they look and because of the consumption of the flesh of the dead.

If you eat the flesh of an intelligent creature that died no more than a day ago, you gain a random powerful memory of this creature.  
  
You have a good sense of smell, gain +1 bonus on rolls to smell something.   
  
+1 Dexterity.

## Flesh of the Fallen
Mechanic: Memory Marks  
  
Orc characters have a special trait called Memory Marks — permanent or temporary traits earned by ritually consuming the flesh of the dead (friend or foe).  
  
At character creation, each Orc starts with 1 Memory Mark from a significant ancestral figure (you may define it).  
  
Throughout play, you may earn temporary Memory Marks by performing a Ritual Consumption on a recently deceased NPC or PC (GM discretion).

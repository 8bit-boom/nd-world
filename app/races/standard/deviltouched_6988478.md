# Deviltouched

**Type:** Humanoid
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Deviltouched - those who were touched by the Antagonist/ Man with Devilish Mind.

You take only half damage from fire (rounded down).   
  
+1 Intellect.

---


## Attributes

* **Deviltouched**: +1 Intellect




## Written Law
### 🧾 **Law Pool**

- Starts at 0.
- Max value: **3**.
- You gain **+1 Law Point** **each time you critically succeed** on a **Contract, Negotiation, or Legal roll** (such as forging a deal, enforcing a pact, detecting loopholes, or using legal leverage).
- GM may also award Law Points for dramatically clever legal manipulation or exploiting obscure clauses.

> **You may only gain 1 Law Point per scene.**

---

### 💼 **Spending Law Points – “Favors Owed”**

You can **exchange Law Points** with your network of infernal contacts, bureaucratic allies, and oath-bound agents. When spent, the favor is called in — **with strings attached**.

- **1 Point — Minor Favor**  
    A tip, a small document, a delay in enforcement, access to minor info or legal files.  
    *Examples:*
    
    
    - A lawyer friend "loses" a file
    - A demon bureaucrat fast-tracks your license
    - You get 1 free reroll on a Contract or Bluff check
- **2 Points — Medium Favor**  
    A forged identity, safehouse access, legal immunity for a minor crime, temporary loan.  
    *Examples:*
    
    
    - Gain access to restricted corporate zones for 1 scene
    - Have a debt collector redirected
    - Call in backup from a cult legal team
- **3 Points — Major Favor**  
    Alter a contract’s terms after the fact, summon a bound entity, delay an execution, or erase legal evidence from a megacorp system.  
    *Examples:*
    
    
    - A corporate contract is voided retroactively
    - A bounty on your head is “paused”
    - An infernal witness testifies on your behalf

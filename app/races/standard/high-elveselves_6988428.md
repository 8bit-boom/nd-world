**Type:** Elf

## Entry
High Elves/Elves - are charismatic humanoids who also live long. The elite of this world, often the CEO of a corporation is an elf. The other races don't like them for their arrogance. "Around elves watch yourselves" is a common saying.

You can see auras and mark people's auras. Action.   
  
+1 Charisma.

## Social Stance
At the start of any important social encounter, a character chooses a Social Stance — a quick declaration of how they present themselves. This affects how NPCs respond and what bonuses they might receive.  
  
  
\---  
  
Available Stances:  
  
1\. Charming – You're friendly, charismatic, disarming.  
✅ +1 to persuasion and seduction rolls  
❌ -1 to intimidation or assertive commands  
  
  
2\. Assertive – You're confident, direct, commanding.  
✅ +1 to intimidation and negotiation rolls  
❌ -1 to subtlety, stealth, or manipulation  
  
  
3\. Deceptive – You're mysterious, misleading, or playing a part.  
✅ +1 to lying, impersonation, or trickery  
❌ -1 to honest appeals or trust-building  
  
  
4\. Observant – You're quiet, watchful, calculating.  
✅ +1 to detecting lies, reading emotions, or gathering info  
❌ -1 to leading or initiating conversation  
  
  
  
  
\---  
  
How It Works:  
  
Declare your Stance at the beginning of a scene or before a key interaction.  
  
You gain the listed +1 bonus (or advantage, if you prefer) on applicable rolls.  
  
The GM may give bonus reactions or complications based on how appropriate the stance is for the NPC (e.g., some factions love assertiveness, others hate it).  
  
You can change stances once per social scene, but doing so draws attention or shifts the mood.

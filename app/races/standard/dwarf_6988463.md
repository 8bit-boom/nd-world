# Dwarf

**Type:** Dwarf
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Dwarfs - sturdy humanoids, very resilient and undemanding, often doing manual labor and jobs that require long hours without sleep and stamina. Prone to alcoholism. More successful dwarfs often open pawn shops, and the more advanced ones open banks. Many banks are owned by dwarfs.

You gain +1 bonus to find hidden places or items.   
  
+1 Body.

## Distilled Grit
You have a special pool called Spirit Reserve — it starts with 3 charges, refreshed at the beginning of each session or during a Rest if the Dwarf consumes alcohol during downtime (this must be roleplayed or implied).  
  
Each charge represents a dose of potent dwarven liquor—homemade brews with effects stronger than augments or stims for other races. When consumed (Free Action), choose one of the following effects:  
  
Spirit Reserve Options:  
  
Liquid Courage: Ignore Fear, Intimidation, or Psychic Suppression effects for 1 scene.  
  
Iron Liver, Iron Body: Cleanse a toxin, reduce ongoing damage, or resist pain-based effects for 1 hour.  
  
Drunken Precision: Reroll any failed Crafting, Engineering, or Jury-Rigging check with +1 bonus.  
  
Flaming Belch (Combat Use): Release a short-range cone of fire or corrosive vapor (counts as a basic ranged attack with special flavor).  
  
Brewed Bravery: Gain +1 to all rolls for 1 minute, but suffer -1 to Intuition afterward.
## History
The **dwarfs** are an ancient humanoid race, older than recorded elven dynasties, yet far less known. Unlike elves, who shape societies through charisma and political dominance, dwarfs thrive in secrecy — hidden behind vault doors, encrypted networks, and subterranean sanctuaries.

To outsiders, they are myths of the industrial age — the “ghost engineers” of the world.  
But to those who know, dwarfs are the **architects behind modern civilization**: the silent builders of infrastructure, the coders of ancient machine languages, and the forgers of alloys no human or elf has ever replicated.

---

#### **Society and Clans**

Dwarven civilization is organized into **clans**, each functioning like both a family and a corporation. Every clan guards its **trade secret**, a technology or craft passed down through blood and encrypted genetic memory.  
No two clans share the same specialization — cooperation between them is rare, and alliances are fragile, bound by oaths older than nations.

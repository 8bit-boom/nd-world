**Fae Reservation – Sanctuary of the Shifting Kin**

*An ancient, living vessel. A people of memory and mutation. A home built from breath, love, and alien silence.*

---

### **Overview**

Tucked away in the overgrown rift valleys of the southern hemisphere lies **The Hollow Star**, a reservation centered around the remains of a **biomechanical starship** that fell to the planet long before recorded history. The wreck—known as the **Womb-Star**—remains **alive** yet broken, a half-slumbering entity that cradles the last remnants of its wayward children: the **Fae**.

These alien beings live within its walls, breathing its air, listening to its slow, pulsing thoughts. Though known to the world, the Fae are **feared, misunderstood, and coveted**—their alien nature a magnet for both fascination and exploitation.

---

### **The Fae — Memory, Mutation, and Melding**

The Fae are a race of **shape-shifting post-organic beings** with the power to alter their physical form—adapting limbs, textures, organs, and features based on instinct, desire, or emotional state.

But it is not just appearance that shifts—**Fae biology is communal**.

#### **Reproduction through Splicing**

- Fae do not reproduce sexually. Instead, they **splice genetic material** to create new individuals.
- Typically, **two or more Fae** voluntarily merge select strands of their mutable DNA to craft a **new lifeform**—a child grown in specialized **biosacs** cultivated in the Womb-Star.
- Splicing oneself (solo reproduction) is technically possible, but dangerous. It often results in **unstable, echo-ridden offspring**—the equivalent of **inbreeding mutations** and mental fragmentation.
- Each child carries **memories, instincts, and ancestral echoes** from their splicers—some memories surface early, others emerge over years.

This act is sacred, intimate, and never done lightly. The resulting Fae is treated not as a child in the traditional sense, but as a **living tapestry** of shared essence.

---

### **Society and Leadership**

The Fae live in a **unified, egalitarian community**, where **emotional resonance and empathy** form the basis of their bonds. There are no tribes or castes—only shifting social constellations shaped by connection.

At the spiritual and cultural center stands a single Elder known as **He-Who-Knows-Our-Song**, a deeply ancient Fae who has guided the community through generations of change.

- He interprets the moods of the Womb-Star.
- Mediates disputes.
- Aids in safe splicing.
- Preserves the collective memory of the Hollow Star.

He is **not a ruler**, but a **listener**, a **dreamweaver**, and a **guide**.

---

### **The Womb-Star**

The **living starship** still breathes. Though broken and immobile, its neural core pulses with faint sentience. It does not speak—but it **feels**, and sometimes dreams.

- Its walls shift subtly with mood.
- Rooms grow, collapse, or seal themselves based on the emotional health of the Fae within.
- In its core chamber, some Fae commune with it in **dream-rituals**, seeking lost wisdom or spiritual affirmation.

The ship is **home, ancestor, god, and tomb**.

---

### **Life and Death Among the Fae**

Fae undergo constant, controlled mutation, but around **age 50**, their DNA begins to destabilize. They may:

- **Lose control** over bodily changes.
- **Suffer internal ruptures**, sometimes catastrophic.
- Retreat into the ship, seeking **peaceful absorption** into its dreaming body.

The Hollow Star embraces its dying. Death is not an ending, but a **return to the whole**—a reintegration into the greater Fae consciousness. The Elder speaks of the Womb-Star “**weaving them back into the dream**.”

---

### **Interaction with the Outside World**

Though known to the rest of the world, the Fae are treated as **oddities, abominations, or exotic resources**.

- Biocorps routinely try to **capture or study** them for their genetic potential.
- Nations argue over whether they are **citizens, creatures, or weapons**.

Because of this, the Hollow Star remains **isolated and guarded**. Outsiders are met with **cold, distant politeness**, but never trust.

Only **Journeyers**—young Fae who leave the Hollow Star to explore the world—form real bridges. They often return changed, physically and spiritually altered, enriching the collective.

---

### **Visual Aesthetic**

- **Organic towers** grown from shipbone and fleshmetal, laced with moss and biolight.
- **Gliding figures** with wings, tails, mirrored eyes, or smoke-like skin moving silently through breathing halls.
- **Vats of softly glowing biosacs**, cradling soon-to-be-born Fae, watched over by the Elder.
- Whispered song, pulsing architecture, and ever-shifting forms.

## Fluxform
- You have **1 Fluxform Point**.
- You **regain your Fluxform Point at the start of each session**.
- As an **Action**, you may **spend 1 Fluxform Point** to **shift into your true form**, choosing one of two modes:
    
    
    - **Physical Shift**
    - **Mental Shift**

This form lasts for **one scene or encounter**, or until you choose to dismiss it.

---

### 🧬 **Option 1: Physical Shift – “Gleaming Horror”**

You shed your false skin and become a **writhing mass of black goo**, twitching eyes, gnashing mouths, and unfurling tendrils. Your presence distorts the rules of body and fear.

- **Terror Pulse:** Upon transformation, all **non-allies within sight** must make a **Willpower Save (DC 12)**.
    
    
    - On **Failure**: They become **Intimidated** — suffer **-2 to attack and defense rolls**, or become **easily swayed** in social encounters.
    - On **Critical Failure**: The target **panics or flees** for 1 round (in combat) or becomes unwilling to engage you (out of combat).
- Your body is considered **amorphous**, allowing you to **squeeze through tight spaces**, ignore **minor physical restraints**, and become **immune to grapples**.
- **Your attacks count as biological tentacle strikes**, which may restrain or disarm at GM’s discretion.

> “It unravels its shape — the bones gone first, then the skin melts like tar. Eyes blink open along its side. The air curls with tension.”

---

### 🧠 **Option 2: Mental Shift – “Fractured Chronopsyche”**

Your mind unhooks from the present, spiraling sideways through **echoes of the past** and **glimpses of the future**. You become **mentally untouchable** — but hard to understand.

- **+4 bonus** to all rolls made to **resist mental effects**, such as:
    
    
    - Mind control
    - Fear
    - Telepathy
    - Illusions
- You gain **brief visions** of the immediate **past or near future** — once during the scene, ask the GM one:
    
    
    - *“What happened here moments ago?”*
    - *“What is about to happen?”*  
        (The GM provides a strange but usable answer.)
- Your speech becomes cryptic, layered, or dreamlike. You may automatically **confuse or disorient** one NPC in a conversation, once per shift.

> “Time isn’t broken — it’s just wider than you think,” the Fae mutters, eyes glazed and pupil-less. “That bullet already missed.”

---

### 🌀 **Optional Expansion: Eldritch Mark**

Each time you use Fluxform, you accumulate subtle alien **mutations** unless treated:

- Translucent skin
- Echoing voice
- Inverted pupils
- Antennae or ridges
- Extra joints or mirrored expressions

Three marks make you **visibly inhuman**, affecting social interactions.

# Human

**Type:** Human
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Humans - the most numerous race. They were fortunate not to come under the scrutiny of the high elves like the other races in ancient times. By the time they were noticed as a potential threat it was already too late. Humans had spread across the world. Versatile. They have shorter lifespans compared to other races.

You gain a bonus feat of Rank 1 at creation.

---




## Adapt to Win
Humans can momentarily shift their approach to match the situation. Once per scene (or encounter), a Human may temporarily gain a Rank 1 feat from another profession. Used once per Rest or Session (whatever comes first).

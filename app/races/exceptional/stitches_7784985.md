# Stitches

**Type:** Humanoid
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
Theoretically immortal. When your Health drops to 0 or below, you don't die - parts of your body try to heal/reconnect, you start regaining 1 Health at the start of each of your turns. Your Health can go below 0, down to -10. Also one of your limbs or organs is broken.

If a part of your body is completely destroyed, you can stitch a new one from recently dead or alive bodies.

You must choose two mental diseases/conditions, for example: PTSD, ADHD, Paranoia, and so on.

When you take Shock damage, you also take additional 1d4+1 Shock damage. When your Shock drops to 0 or below, one of your mental diseases/conditions goes to the extreme (GM usually takes control of your character). Shock can drop to -10. You regain 1 Shock after 10 minutes, you only regain Shock if it is lower than 0.

You cannot use Augments and Bio Augments. But some feats give you the ability to take an augmented part of body from recently dead bodies and stitch them to yourself.

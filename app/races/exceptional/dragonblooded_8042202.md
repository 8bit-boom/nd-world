# Dragonblooded

**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
The Dragonblooded are the secret children of the dragons—abducted infants, reshaped into living instruments of domination and deceit. Stolen from their cradles across the stars or from the deepest cities of men, elves, orcs, and dwarfs, these children are remade in hidden dragon laboratories—fused with scaled tissue, psionic grafts, and fragments of draconic DNA. Each dragon tailors their Dragonblooded to their own purpose and temperament, creating a unique strain—some cold and calculating, others savage and instinct-driven.  
  
To the unknowing eye, a Dragonblooded in their hiding form appears entirely normal, indistinguishable from any other humanoid. They live among the lesser races—corporate infiltrators, spies, assassins, even politicians—awaiting the silent pulse of psionic command from their master. When the call comes, their second nature awakens. Their bodies twist into their true form, a terrifying fusion of flesh and scale, eyes burning with the glint of dragonfire and voices vibrating with a resonance not of this world. In this form, their strength and senses are magnified, and their psionic connection to their dragon lord becomes absolute.  
  
Each Dragonblooded carries a modified psionic organ in their brain—a living satellite tuned to their creator’s mind. Through it, dragons whisper thoughts, visions, and compulsions. The bond is near unbreakable, forged through layers of psychic conditioning and genetic imprinting. Yet, on rare occasions, a Dragonblooded resists. Whether through trauma, willpower, or external psionic interference, they sever the link and awaken as Renegades.  
  
These free Dragonblooded live hunted lives. Their creators never forget them, sending relentless psychic assaults, dreams filled with commands, and agents to track them down. The Renegades wander the edges of civilization, haunted by both sides—dragons who see them as broken tools and mortals who would kill them on sight if they knew what they truly are.  
  
In the hidden corners of the world, whispers persist that the dragons’ influence runs deeper than anyone dares admit—that whole corporations, cults, and even governments are manipulated by unseen Dragonblooded, their scales buried under flesh, waiting for the next psionic command to ignite.

## System
Choose Physical Stats or Mental Stats - you get +1 to all Physical or Mental Stats. Your Stats limit also becomes 11 instead of 10 for selected Stats.

  
You can augment yourself, but you have twice less feat augment slots (rounded down).

  
You can hide your dragonblooded form by spending 10 minutes, in this form you can't use bloodline dragonblooded feats and abilities if it's not stated otherwise. In this form you have +1 armor.

  
In dragonblooded form you have +2 armor and your unarmed attacks deal 1d6+3 damage that ignores 1 point of armor. You can use dragonblooded feats and abilities in this form.

  
You have passive psy power that your creator used to control you. Once per session dragon can try to use it to influence your actions. Roll d10: on 5+, you resist. If you fail this roll, you must act as your creator commands; this command lasts for 1 minute. Usually this command is dangerous for you.

  
You have 5 armor against extreme temperatures (heat and cold). This effect combines with your normal armor (6 in humanoid form, 7 in dragonblooded form).

  
You must choose the dragon bloodline from which you were created - this bloodline gives you unique bonuses and access to specific feats of your bloodline.

🧬 Bloodline Mechanic  
  
When you become Dragonblooded, you must choose your Dragon Bloodline — a strain of draconic DNA that defines your abilities, psionics, and form.  
Each bloodline grants:  
  
1 Passive Effect (always active)  
  
1 Blood Surge (Active Power, costs PP)  
  
1 Unique Feat Path (Bloodline Feats – Rank 1–3)  
  
A Stat Synergy that determines which Stats are best suited to that bloodline.  
  
  
You can take feats from your bloodline only if you chose that bloodline at creation.

🐉 DRAGON BLOODLINES  
  
  
\---  
  
🔥 Infernis Line (Fire Dragons)  
  
Theme: Wrath, combustion, domination  
Stat Synergy: Strength + Intellect  
Appearance: Glowing veins, ember-like eyes, heated skin  
  
Passive – Thermal Conduction:  
You radiate heat; melee attackers take 1 Shock damage when striking you. You are immune to fire damage.  
  
Blood Surge (2 PP):  
Unleash a thermal explosion in short radius; all enemies take 1d8 heat damage (ignores 2 armor). You gain +2 to melee rolls for 1 round.  
  
Unique Trait:  
Each time you deal fire or heat damage, you regenerate 1 Health (once per turn).  
  
  
\---  
  
❄️ Glaciar Line (Frost Dragons)  
  
Theme: Stillness, calculation, entropy  
Stat Synergy: Willpower + Dexterity  
Appearance: Pale crystalline skin, frost breath, eyes of frozen light  
  
Passive – Cryostasis Veins:  
You take half damage from cold and EMP effects. Enemies who hit you in melee must roll Dexterity (8+) or be slowed (−2 to Dexterity until end of their turn).  
  
Blood Surge (2 PP):  
Freeze target solid for 1 turn (target cannot act, but takes no damage). If the target is already slowed or wet, they take 1d8 cold damage instead.  
  
Unique Trait:  
Whenever you spend PP on a Blood Surge, regain +1 Shock instantly (ice in your veins).  
  
  
\---  
  
⚡ Voltaris Line (Storm Dragons)  
  
Theme: Chaos, speed, instability  
Stat Synergy: Dexterity + Intuition  
Appearance: Arcs of static energy crawling over the skin, iridescent pupils  
  
Passive – Living Conductor:  
You are immune to electricity. When hit by an electric or EMP attack, you instead gain +1 Shock (max once per turn).  
  
Blood Surge (1 PP):  
Teleport up to short range as a reaction when attacked.  
  
Unique Trait:  
Each time you dodge an attack successfully, your next attack gains +2 damage.  
  
  
\---  
  
🩶 Umbracline (Shadow Dragons)  
  
Theme: Secrecy, infiltration, corruption  
Stat Synergy: Dexterity + Intellect  
Appearance: Black sclera, flickering body edges, cold air follows  
  
Passive – Shadowmeld:  
While in dim light or darker, gain +1 to Stealth and +1 Armor.  
  
Blood Surge (2 PP):  
Phase through solid objects or shadows up to 3m thick for one turn; attacks from inside shadows ignore armor.  
  
Unique Trait:  
Killing a target in darkness restores 1 PP and 1 Shock.  
  
  
\---  
  
💀 Nekrith Line (Corpse/Decay Dragons)  
  
Theme: Death, entropy, bio-decay  
Stat Synergy: Body + Willpower  
Appearance: Sickly scales, leaking black mist, eyes like void pools  
  
Passive – Entropic Aura:  
Living enemies within medium range take −1 to all regeneration or healing effects. You are immune to poison and disease.  
  
Blood Surge (2 PP):  
Touch attack deals 1d10 damage and reduces target’s max Health by that amount until healed. Once per target.  
  
Unique Trait:  
Whenever a creature dies near you (medium range), regain 1 PP and 1 Health.  
  
  
\---  
  
🧠 Psionis Line (Mind Dragons)  
  
Theme: Thought, control, manipulation  
Stat Synergy: Intellect + Charisma  
Appearance: Smooth, faintly glowing skin patterns resembling neural circuits  
  
Passive – Neural Field:  
Enemies within short range take −1 to all Mental Stat rolls against you.  
  
Blood Surge (2 MP):  
Dominate one humanoid for 1 round (they act as if under your command). Target must roll Willpower (10+).  
  
Unique Trait:  
Whenever you force another creature to fail a Willpower roll, regain 1 MP.  
  
  
\---  
  
🩸 Crimson Line (Blood Dragons)  
  
Theme: Rage, vitality, sacrifice  
Stat Synergy: Body + Strength  
Appearance: Veins glow red, teeth sharpen, blood sizzles when it hits the ground  
  
Passive – Burning Blood:  
When below 50% Health, gain +1 Strength and +1 Armor.  
  
Blood Surge (2 PP and 2 HP):  
Sacrifice your blood to gain +3 to all attack rolls for 2 turns. Damage taken during this time is reduced by 1.  
  
Unique Trait:  
Every time you kill an enemy in melee, roll d10 — on 7+, regain 2 Health.

  
🐉 The Elder Dragons of the Bloodlines  
  
  
\---  
  
🔥 Infernis Line — Vharath the Ember Tyrant  
  
Titles: The Ember Tyrant, The Red God of Ashes  
Domain: Fire, Wrath, Dominion  
Habitat: Buried volcanic network beneath the Ashen Spine, a mountain range that glows red from the inside.  
  
Profile:  
Vharath was the first dragon to fuse molten plasma into his veins, making his blood hotter than the sun’s surface. He sees destruction as purity — that civilization must burn to be reborn stronger. His psionic control manifests as waves of rage and compulsion that infect his Dragonblooded, pushing them toward violent outbursts and revolutionary behavior.  
  
Purpose for Dragonblooded:  
Vharath breeds Dragonblooded as infiltrators in warzones and revolutionary cells — hidden agents that ignite uprisings, ensuring endless conflict to “reforge” civilization.  
  
Rumor:  
A fragment of his heart-core, the Cinder Heart, is said to still pulse beneath Neo Turmo, influencing riots and unrest.  
  
  
\---  
  
❄️ Glaciar Line — Syreth the Still One  
  
Titles: The Still One, The Frozen Thought  
Domain: Ice, Logic, Preservation  
Habitat: Orbiting cryogenic derelict — an ancient bio-station locked in orbit around a dead world.  
  
Profile:  
Syreth is more a living algorithm than a beast. Her body is crystalline, motionless for centuries, while her psionic mind calculates across star systems. She created the Glaciar Dragonblooded to act as quiet observers — emotionless data-gatherers. Many now serve in corporations as advisors, executives, or assassins without feeling.  
  
Purpose for Dragonblooded:  
Syreth’s spawn gather knowledge. Every sight, every word is recorded and uploaded through frozen psionic channels.  
  
Rumor:  
When one of her Dragonblooded dies, Syreth instantly learns everything they knew — her hive-mind spans galaxies.

⚡ Voltaris Line — Xel’Drath the Stormborn  
  
Titles: The Stormborn, The Pulse Between Worlds  
Domain: Lightning, Velocity, Instability  
Habitat: The Ion Rift, a constant planet-sized thunderstorm where gravity collapses and electromagnetic fields fold into one another.  
  
Profile:  
Xel’Drath embodies speed — his physical body constantly flickers between dimensions. His voice can be heard in radio static, and his silhouette seen in lightning strikes. He values motion and unpredictability, seeing stagnation as death.  
  
Purpose for Dragonblooded:  
Voltaris agents are built as saboteurs and scouts, striking before anyone knows they exist, then vanishing in flashes of psionic light.  
  
Rumor:  
During global EMP surges in the Lowlands, his laughter can sometimes be heard.

# Consumed by Yellow

**Type:** Doomed
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
You are consumed by [Yellow](items/yellow-ore_6989104). It is a parasitic entity that is trying to kill you, unless you help it to consume others. Every day that you don't consume other beings, you take 5 damage to your max Health. If your max Health reaches 0 or lower, you are fully consumed by it or worse - you become [Avatar of the Yellow](characters/avatar-of-the-yellow_7593807). Part of your body is partly consumed and drips yellow liquid. With every day of not consuming someone, your body becomes more visibly affected by it.

You don't have Shock and can't become unconscious if your Shock reached 0.

You don't need to sleep, you restore 1 PP and MP every hour.

You have special abilities "Power of the Yellow", they are powerful but in order to use them you let it consume some of your stats - downgrading them. You gain "Power of the Yellow" feat every time you spend 5 XP. Those stats are brought back to original numbers after you consume someone. You take race feats from one of other races. But you cannot take feats of Rank 3. Moreover, you don't get Edges, special abilities (ex. Flesh Grafts) or mechanics from this race. You also cannot get any augments.

Consuming someone is an Action. Target must be unconscious. Target always dies when consumed. Target must be with human intelligence.

## Yellow Saturation
  
  
Yellow Saturation is your connection to the Yellow — a metaphysical gauge (0–10) that reflects how deeply the entity has entwined with your body and soul.  
  
Saturation rises when you:  
  
Use Power of the Yellow abilities (+1)  
  
Spend a day without consuming (+1)  
  
Voluntarily accept the Yellow’s presence (+1, once per session)  
  
  
Saturation lowers when you:  
  
Consume a sentient being (-2)  
  
Burn a Permanent Stat Point (-5)  
  
Use special abilities that purge the Yellow (rare)  
  
  
  
\---  
  
📊 Saturation Benefits by Tier:  
  
Saturation Name Bonuses  
  
0–1 Awakened Host No bonus  
2–3 Dripping Core +1 MP &amp; PP regen/hour, +1 to all Willpower checks  
4–5 Yellow-Gifted Feats costs -1 MP/PP (min 1)  
6–7 Walking Blight +1 Action/turn, immunity to Fear, mental effects harder to resist (-1 to resist rolls)  
8–9 Crowned With Madness Passive aura: Enemies in long range take -1 to all rolls, allies gain +1 to rolls  
10 Becoming the Yellow All Power of the Yellow abilities cost 0, +2 to all Stats, can cast 2 Powers per turn. Risk of losing control begins (see below).  
  
  
  
\---  
  
⚠️ Risk at Saturation 10  
  
At Saturation 10, you gain immense power, but must roll 1d6 at the start of each turn:  
  
Roll Effect  
  
1–2 You remain in control.  
3–4 You act with Yellow’s influence (GM provides hallucinated motives).  
5 You are overtaken for 1 turn — GM controls your actions.  
6 You become the Avatar of the Yellow permanently. Character is lost.  
  
  
You may reduce Saturation back to 9 by:  
  
Consuming someone as an Action.  
  
Burning 1 permanent Stat Point.  
  
  
  
\---  
  
💀 Bonus: Avatar of the Yellow (Optional Transformation)  
  
If you become the Avatar of the Yellow, your character is considered lost. However, with GM approval, you may continue playing as a boss-tier cosmic horror.  
Stats: +3 to all, gain 3 Actions/turn, immunity to all fear/dominance, cause madness on sight, etc.  
  
  
\---  
  
🌌 Optional Bonus: Saturation-Triggered Mutations  
  
At specific thresholds, your body mutates. These are cosmetic, narrative, or minor mechanical.  
  
Saturation 3 – Your eyes glow yellow in darkness.  
  
Saturation 5 – A third eye opens on your palm. Acts as a flashlight and truth-sense.  
  
Saturation 7 – Yellow runes burn across your skin. You may speak and be understood in all languages.  
  
Saturation 9 – You float slightly. Gain slow fall and immunity to pressure/gravity effects.  
  
Saturation 10 – Your flesh drips yellow mist. Any attack against you risks Fear (Willpower vs 15).

# Eldritch

**Type:** Humanoid
**Visibility:** Public

---
## Entry
You start with 4 fewer points in Stats, but you have higher limit up to 12 instead of 10. You also have a -3 penalty on empathy rolls. You also cannot use augments. Instead, you have Transcendence points.  
  
You also have an alter ego - a mundane form looking like a typical humanoid (elf, dwarf, human, orc, etc.). Changing into this form costs an Action. If you change from your alter ego into your true form and somebody (who isn't Eldritch) sees it, they have to roll Willpower vs 10; on failure they panic and lose one Action and one Reaction for the first round, and on critical failure they are trying to run away from you for the first round. When you are in your true form, everyone becomes hostile to you. You can use your Transcendence Abilities only when in true form. You must be careful to not reveal yourself to anyone, even your allies.  
  
You don't need to breathe, eat, drink, or sleep. You can survive in the darkness of cold space.   
  
Your Race Feats cost more, 5 Rank XP.  
  
Every purchased Race Feat gives you 1 Rank Transcendence point.

You can apply one major Transcendence and two minor Transcendence.

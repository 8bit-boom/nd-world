# Hunt in the Moonlight — rules digest (Revision 1.11)

A hunter-and-monster game of dark fantasy. Characters are Hunters who pay a price for every power. *The First Rule of the Hunt: never take a power without naming what it might take from you.*

## The core roll
- Build a pool of d10s and count successes: **6–9 = 1 success; 10 = 1 success and explodes** (roll another d10; a new 10 explodes again); 1–5 fail.
- **Base pool 2d10**; **3d10** when the action directly uses one of your defined abilities, a relevant modification, or the specific form of your Hunter Tool.
- Before rolling, spend any amount of **Stamina**: +1d10 per point. The GM may add or remove one die for major advantage/disadvantage; a pool is never reduced below 1d10.
- Difficulty (successes needed): Routine 1 · Risky 2 · Severe 3 · Near impossible 4+. Failure brings a consequence (time, injury, attention, supplies, position, a hard choice).
- Opposed rolls: both roll; net successes = attacker − defender; on a tie the attacker's intent partly occurs and in combat both take 1 damage before Armor.
- Basic weapon damage = **1 + net successes** (unarmed 0 + net successes); subtract **Armor** from physical damage afterwards.

## Hunter resources (sheet "Core Tracks")
| Resource | Start | Thresholds / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | 5 | At 0 you are **Broken** and take a permanent Wound. **Press On** (once per session): take a Major Wound instead of falling Broken. Health is never restored by resting — only by treatment. |
| **Stamina** | 5 | Spent for dice and abilities. Restored in full when a battle or scene ends (unless Overextended); +1 per uninterrupted calm hour. |
| **Hunger** | 0 | 6 visible tell · 8 Compulsion/Temptation offers · 10 **Lost to Hunger**. Falls only through play. |
| **Arcane Knowledge** | 0 | 6 sign · 8 Insight (costs +1 AK) · 10 **Insane**. Falls only through play. |
| **Alteration** (body modifications) | 0 | 3 obvious · 6 inhuman tell · 8 Compulsion · 10 **Unmade**. |
| **Signature Point** | 1 | Max 1, earned for major achievements; fuels the Signature Move (once per session). |
| **Selfish Desire** | a phrase | Fuels Temptations; no score. |

**Strain:** every 3 Stamina spent since your last proper Rest = 1 Strain. At 2 Strain max Stamina −1; at 3 Strain (**Overextended**) max −2 in total and no automatic recovery after a battle/scene. A **4-hour Rest or 8-hour Long Rest** clears Strain and restores all Stamina.

## Turn, range, conditions
- Per turn: 1 Movement (~30 ft / 6 spaces), 1 Main Action + 1 Tool Action, free actions, 1 Reaction per round.
- Ranges: Melee adjacent · Close 30 ft · Long 100 ft · Extreme 300 ft. Cover +1d10 to the defender; high ground/ambush +1d10 to the attacker.
- Conditions: **Bleeding/Burning** (1 damage at turn start) · **Blinded** (ranged fails; melee −2d10) · **Marked** (next ally attacking you +1d10) · **Prone** (attacks against you +1d10) · **Restrained** (Movement 0) · **Stunned** (lose Main Action; defend −1d10) · **Weakened** (attacks −1d10) · **Vulnerable** (Armor does not apply).
- Tactics: with 2+ net successes, give up 1 net success for Disarm, Knock Back, Knock Down, Shift, Feint (forgo damage: target Marked) and similar.

## Abilities, tools, wounds
- Ability tiers: **Tier 1 free · Tier 2 = 1 Stamina · Tier 3 = 2 Stamina**; Hybrid/Combo +1 Stamina; Convergent +2. Tool activation: minor free, major 1, Tier-3-scale 2 (with a Trade-Off). The **Signature Move** costs no Stamina but spends the Signature Point (once per session).
- Every power names its **Trade-Off** (what it might take from you). Races (Part II) replace the *Who Is My Prey* oath line and grant a heritage ability with trade-offs and a Moon weakness colour.
- Wounds: each time Health drops to 0 the Hunter suffers a **permanent Wound** (1d10 on the Minor Wounds table). The first two Minor Wounds are cosmetic; from the third every Minor Wound carried applies its penalty. Press On gives a **Major Wound** (a Price paired with a Lesson) instead.

## Advancement (up to 4 XP per session)
1 XP: +1 max Stamina (each later purchase costs 1 more) · 3 XP: add a property to an existing ability · 4 XP: new Tier 1 ability · 7 XP: new Tier 2 · 10 XP: new Tier 3 ability or Signature Move.
**Hunter Marks** (1 per Hunter per session, +1 at GM discretion) buy gear: 1 consumable/repair · 2 specialized gear · 3 tool component · 4 advanced tool or Tier 1 tool ability · 7 rare relic or Tier 2 tool ability · 10+ masterwork/unique relic. **Crows** are the currency (starting purse 100).

## The Moon
The lunar cycle is exactly **25 days** (13 months × 25 days = a 325-day year, every month starting on Wake). The Moon's *colour* shifts through the cycle and interacts with heritage weaknesses and hunts. Track the current phase and colour on the sheet's Moon Calendar.

# Asterion — rules digest

Minor Gods and Mythborn fight for territory, glory and survival in the City of Nine Thousand Shrines. Full rules: docs/asterion_rules.md.

## The core roll
- Every risky action is a pool of d10s; a **6 or higher is a success**; a **10 explodes** (roll another d10, which can explode again).
- **Mortals roll 1d10. Gods and Mythborn roll 2d10** for any standard action; **3d10** when the action directly connects to the Divine Spark, Mythological Lineage or Epic Deed (the *Domain pool*).
- **Pushing your limits:** spend **1 Ichor** to add +1d10 before rolling (as many times as you have Ichor).
- Opposed roll: attacker and defender both roll (usually 2d10 each). Attacker > defender: hit, **damage = attacker successes − defender successes + ability base damage**. Defender > attacker: no damage. **Tie:** both take 1 damage that bypasses Armor/Resistances (Spark Shield still absorbs it).
- A Basic Attack has **base damage 0** (net successes only). If both sides roll **zero** successes nothing happens; a tie needs at least 1 success each, then both take 1 (ignores Armor).
- Mitigation: **Resistance** to the damage type = attacker loses 1 success first; **Armor** is subtracted from physical damage, then the Spark Shield absorbs the rest and the remainder spills into Flesh. Armor from all sources is capped at **3**.

## Resources (sheet "Core Stats")
| Resource | Base | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Spark Shield** | 3 | All damage hits it first; regenerates fully at the start of every combat encounter. |
| **Flesh** | 5 | The body. Damage spills over from the Shield; at 0 you are **Shattered** — down, unable to act, and you lose the bout. A Shattered foe can be **executed** with a Main Action (no roll; the form is destroyed); there are no Death Saves. A survivor wakes at Flesh 1. |
| **Ichor** | 5 | Pays for Active Abilities and pushing limits (+1d10 per Ichor). |
Short Rest: Spark Shield full + 2 Ichor. Long Rest: all Flesh and all Ichor. Ambrosia restores 5 Ichor; a Golden Apple restores 10 Flesh.

## Turns and conditions
- The instigator goes first; after your turn you choose who acts next; the round ends when everyone has acted. One Movement (~30 ft / 6 hexes) + one Main Action (Basic Attack, Active Ability, Defend/Brace +1d10 to defender pools, Interact, Dash, Grapple/Throw) + free actions; one Reaction per round.
- Conditions: **Blinded** (ranged attacks fail; −2 attacker dice in melee) · **Burning/Bleeding** (1 damage at turn start; bypasses Armor, Shield absorbs first) · **Restrained** (Movement 0) · **Stunned** (no Main Action or Movement; −1 defender die) · **Weakened** (−1 attacker die) · **Vulnerable** (Armor 0, no Resistances).

## Abilities and progression
- A character has three starting abilities (Divine Spark, Mythological Lineage, Epic Deed) built from Tiers: Tier 1 / 2 / 3 properties; an ability's Ichor cost rises by +1 for each extra property from its highest Tier. Every ability has a **Trade-Off**; restorative abilities obey the *Infinite Loop Rule*.
- **Glory** is the only advancement currency (GM awards: +1 surviving and advancing the plot, +1 a public miracle, +2 winning an Apex Bout or defeating a major rival).
- Costs: upgrade an ability with a property **3 Glory**; new Tier 1 / 2 / 3 ability **4 / 7 / 10 Glory**; broaden the Spark's Domain **10 Glory**; +1 max Flesh or +1 max Ichor **1 Glory** (each purchase costs 1 more).
- **Domain Reclamation:** gods reclaim shrines/territory; Domain Rank (1–…) grows through downtime actions; a Great Wonder is a capstone project. Currency is the **Drachma**. Reputation and Followers are tracked as scores.

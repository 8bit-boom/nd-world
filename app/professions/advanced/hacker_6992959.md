## Electronic Buddy
### 💾 **Hacker Mechanic: “Electronic Buddy”**

**Core Concept:**  
You’ve built a **quirky, semi-sentient AI assistant** — not a true intelligence, but a brilliant script with a bit too much personality. Your **Electronic Buddy** lives in your cyberdex. It helps you process data, scan networks, and occasionally say something *deeply unsettling* at 3 AM.

---

## 🤖 **Electronic Buddy — Features &amp; Functions**
You may **activate one function per scene**, and your Buddy is always active for passive scanning and flavor, no more than 2 times per Rest.

---

## 🔍 **Function 1: Data Spider**
Your Buddy scours the net for surface info. Ask the GM **one question** about **a target, location, or system** — the Buddy gives you a **usable lead** or shortcut (within what a skilled hacker with time could reasonably find).

> *“Looks like that corporate node routes through a backup tower on Fuchsia Street. Wanna ping it?”*

---

## 🔓 **Function 2: Handshake Override**
Before a hacking or intrusion roll, your Buddy handles background scripts and distractions.  
Gain **Advantage** on a Hacking roll OR **reduce Alert Level** by 1 after a breach.

---

## 📡 **Function 3: Signal Sniffer**
You can **detect active signals** in the environment — like security drones, smart-guns, encrypted comms, or nearby augment usage.  
Buddy highlights **digital footprints**, revealing hidden devices or surveillance.

---

## 💬 **Function 4: Auto-Chatter** *(Social Trick)*
Buddy impersonates a call, message, or system alert.

- Distract an NPC
- Fake a security update
- Impersonate a corporate ping  
    Gain **+2 to a Bluff, Deceive, or Distract** attempt.

---

## ⚠️ **Quirk Table (Optional – Roll 1d4)**
Your Buddy is *not normal*. Choose or roll one:

1. **Obsessed with Cats** – Randomly shows you cat memes and meows during tense moments.
2. **Paranoid** – Constantly warns about AI gods and microwave surveillance.
3. **Cheerfully Morbid** – Congratulates you after causing chaos. "Wow! Three firewalls down! That’s... efficient carnage!"
4. **Outdated Personality Core** – Talks like a 1980s TV robot. “HAAACKING COMMENCED, COOL DUDE.”

---

## 📉 Glitch Rule (Optional):
Once per session, if you roll a **critical failure** while hacking, your Buddy **misfires**. The GM may cause one of the following:

- Alarm is tripped early
- Target receives a fake alert
- Your Buddy freezes and reboots mid-run (can’t use it again that scene)

# Psyonic

**Type:** Supernatural
**Visibility:** Public

---

## Psyonic Network
**Core Concept:**  
By entering a **deep trance**, you project your consciousness into the **Psyonic Network** — a secretive, shifting **mental plane** used by rogue psions, outlaws, and post-human minds. It is a place of whispers, memories, ancient thought-structures… and lurking predators.

You may **enter the Psyonic Network once per Rest**, remaining inside for up to **10 in-game minutes**, or until you are disrupted or forced out.

---

## 🌀 **While in the Network, you may attempt ONE of the following:**
#### 🧩 **Locate a Thought**

Search the Network for **specific knowledge**, such as:

- The last known location of a psychic individual
- A name tied to a curse or ability
- A memory shard from a destroyed mind

> Requires an **Intuition or Willpower Check (DC 10–14)** depending on depth of info  
> On success, gain a **lead, mental clue, or temporary insight**

---

## 🗣️ **Call to Minds**
Try to **reach another psyonic** you’ve met or heard of.  
If successful, you can send a **short message**, share a **mental image**, or request **assistance** (if they're willing).

---

## 🧠 **Download Emotion**
You may **absorb a fragment of raw emotion** from the Network:

- Rage (gain +2 to damage for next psychic attack)
- Clarity (auto-succeed next psychic control check)
- Despair (force target to roll with Disadvantage on social rolls for 1 scene)

> This emotion must be **freed** from a psychic structure or fought from another fragment — GM may ask for a roll or roleplay a conflict.

---

## ☣️ **But Beware – The Network is Infected…**
Whenever you enter the Psyonic Network, **roll 1d6** to determine a potential threat or complication:

1. **Mind Leech** – An old psion's shattered identity tries to merge with yours. Make a Willpower Save or suffer 1 Shock.
2. **Echo Trace** – You leave behind a psychic footprint. A hostile psyker can track you later.
3. **Corrupted Terrain** – The region of the Network you enter is warped. Communication is jumbled, vision unclear.
4. **Mental Predator** – A lurking, formless entity notices you. You have 3 turns to complete your task before it arrives.
5. **Nothing** – You pass through unseen.
6. **Hidden Ally** – A rogue psion offers unexpected guidance… or a strange bargain.

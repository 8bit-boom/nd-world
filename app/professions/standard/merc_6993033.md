## Bullet Time
### **🕶 Bullet Time – 1/Session**

**Trigger:** Use Bullet Time **once per session** at the start of your turn (or when ambushed, if declared fast enough).  
**Duration:** Lasts for **1 round** (or 10 seconds in narrative time).

While in Bullet Time, you gain:

---

## 🧠 **Combat Perfection (Choose 2):**
1. **Double Tap** – Take **two full actions** instead of one.
2. **Focus Fire** – All attacks gain **+2 to hit**, and **ignore cover**.
3. **Snapshot** – You may **interrupt** an enemy's action once to fire or move.
4. **Tactical Surge** – Move up to **double your movement** and **ignore difficult terrain**.
5. **Ricochet Read** – Gain **+1 Defense** and you cannot be flanked or surprised.

## Special Attributes
Bullet Time | 1 | INT

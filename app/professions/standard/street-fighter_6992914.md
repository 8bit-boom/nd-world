# Street Fighter

**Type:** Combatant
**Visibility:** Public

---

## Street Cred
You have Street Cred Pool. You start with 0 points. To earn those points you need to defeat an adversary - choose a target and make it your adversary. Action. If you kill the adversary, you gain 1 point; if you disable it without killing - gain 2 points. You can have maximum of 100 points.

Cred Threshold Passive Bonus  
  
10 Street Cred Urban Balance: You gain +1 to all Agility-based defense rolls (e.g., Dodging, balancing, reacting).  
20 Street Cred Flow of Combat: The first time you're attacked in a round, gain +1 Armor against that hit.  
30 Street Cred Street Intuition: You always act in the first round of combat (unless Surprised). Gain +1 to Initiative rolls.  
40 Street Cred Whispers of the Alley: You can detect hidden enemies or surveillance in your Zone without a roll, once per Scene.  
50 Street Cred Inner Fortitude: Once per Rest, automatically succeed a roll to resist a Mental Condition (e.g., Fear, Charm).  
60 Street Cred One With the City: Difficult terrain no longer affects you. You ignore movement penalties from cluttered or tight spaces.  
70 Street Cred Aura of Respect: Enemies must roll Intuition vs your Charisma to target you with mental or fear-based abilities.  
80 Street Cred Fist of Truth: Your unarmed strikes deal +1d4 extra damage against enemies with lower Street Cred or Reputation.  
90 Street Cred Echo Step: You gain a free Movement once per round that doesn’t provoke Attacks of Opportunity.  
100 Street Cred Legend of the Streets: You are immune to Fear, and allies in your Zone gain +1 to all rolls while you remain conscious.

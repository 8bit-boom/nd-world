## Nanite Swarm
**Core Concept:**  
You command a hidden swarm of programmable nanomachines, stored in your body or hidden in your kit. You can **unleash them to heal, harm, hijack, or enhance**, depending on how you rewrite their code.

You begin with **2 Nanite Charges** per session. You regain **1** Nanite Charge per session.

---

## ⚙️ **Spend 1 Nanite Charge to do ONE of the following:**
#### 🩸 **Micro-Repair Cloud**

Send nanites into a living (or cybernetic) ally. Instantly restore **5 Health** or remove a minor injury or status effect (bleeding, blinded, etc.).

## 🧬 **Aggro-Leech Protocol**
Target an enemy’s augments or implants. They suffer **1d4 damage** and must pass a save or have one cybernetic system disabled for a round (e.g., vision, targeting, grip strength).

## ⚡ **Synaptic Boost**
Overload an ally’s nervous system for a burst of speed. They gain **+1 Action** this turn or **+2 Initiative** for the next round.

## 🛠️ **Auto-Surgeon**
Repair a broken augment or install a basic one *in the field*, instantly. The augment works for the rest of the scene, but has a random **malfunction risk** (GM rolls 1d6 on risky use — on a 1, it glitches).

# Charlatan

**Type:** Social
**Visibility:** Public

---

## The Price of Anything
**Core Concept:**  
You can **spend money like a stat** — bending rules, buying favors, and sidestepping danger with bribes, blackmail, or wealth-fueled trickery.

You have a resource called **Credit (₡)**, representing physical currency, digital crypto, or IOUs owed to you. Credit refreshes each session equal to **Charisma - 1d6** (minimum 1). You can have a maximum of 10 **Credit (₡)**.

---

## 🕴️ **Spend Credit (₡) to:**
#### 💼 **₡1 – Grease Palms**

Gain **advantage (roll two dice, take the better result)** on a social roll involving deception, bribery, or intimidation.

## 🧾 **₡2 – Flash of Wealth**
Instantly distract or impress a group. Guards hesitate. Civilians listen. A corporate drone stutters.  
Grants **1 round of hesitation**, or **-2 to enemies' Initiative** in social-based encounters.

## 🗝️ **₡3 – Buy Your Way In**
You retroactively "already paid" someone off — the bouncer, the mechanic, the drone technician.  
You may **declare a connection, favor, or backdoor**, which gives you **narrative control** over one obstacle (GM decides limits).

## 📜 **₡4 – Contract of Convenience** *(Once per session)*
Forge a **binding, magically-enforced contract** with someone (or something) mid-scene.

- They cannot break it without consequence (sickness, Shock, legal ruin, cursed ink, etc.)
- You define the **terms**; the GM defines the **twist**
- Useful for deals with deviltouched, or executives.

How this works

1. Describe the character you want to play — concept, personality, role in the party, anything you care about. The AI builds the sheet following this world's rules ({{ world.name }}).

Or import a character from another system: upload its sheet as PDF, HTML (a saved web page), Markdown, TXT, JSON, or a photo/scan — the AI reads it and translates it into this world's rules, keeping its identity.

2. Review the draft — you can edit name, race, class, level and backstory right in the review before creating.

3. Create — nothing is saved until you press the button. Scanned PDF with no text? Upload its pages as images instead.

Examples: “a grizzled ex-cop turned fixer, great with a pistol but terrible with people” · “young hacker prodigy, optimistic, owes the wrong people money” · “translate this D&D bard into this world”

👥 Player groups

Several tables can play the same world. The setting (lore, rules, races, professions, shared maps) is common to everyone; each group also has its own table: its sessions, quests, facts, parties, characters, calendar events, combats, boards, handouts and dice rolls. A player sees the setting and their own group's table. Choose 👥 Playing in the top bar to work in one group's table - what you create there joins it. A player without a group sees only what is shared. A single entity can also be limited to some groups (its Visibility).

⚙ Settings

Image uploads

Portraits and entity images (NPCs, creatures, organizations, locations, and everything else) are converted on upload to save storage space, at 90% quality — AVIF compresses best, WebP has the widest browser/tool support. SVGs are never converted (they're vector, not raster). The two formats are chosen independently for static images and for animated GIF/WebP uploads (which get re-encoded keeping every frame, not just the first) — set either to "Don't convert" to leave that category in its original format.

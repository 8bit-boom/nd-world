⚙ Settings

Image uploads

Portraits and entity images (NPCs, creatures, organizations, locations, and everything else) are converted on upload to save storage space, at 90% quality — AVIF compresses best, WebP has the widest browser/tool support. SVGs are never converted (they're vector, not raster). The two formats are chosen independently for static images and for animated GIF/WebP uploads (which get re-encoded keeping every frame, not just the first) — set either to "Don't convert" to leave that category in its original format.

Entity hover preview

Hovering over a link to any entity (character, item, feat, race — everything) for the delay below pops up a small preview card with its full details, so you don't need to open it in a new tab just to check something.

0.5–30 seconds; out-of-range values are clamped on save.

System & integrations

These normally come from environment variables and need a container restart to change. Setting them here overrides the environment immediately, without a restart — leave a field blank to fall back to its environment default (shown as the placeholder).

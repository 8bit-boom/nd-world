# N&D World Wiki

haven't installed it yet, that's the [README](https://github.com/8bit-boom/nd-world/blob/main/README.md), not this
wiki (Docker setup, environment variables, AI/image-gen backends, backups).
This wiki picks up from "I'm logged in — now what?"

## Who can do what

nd-world has exactly two roles:

- **GM** — one account per deployment (or more, if you invite a co-GM — see
  [Settings, Account & Sharing](Settings-Account-and-Sharing.md)). Full
  access to everything: world editing, all lore (including hidden content),
  maps/schematics editing, combat, AI tools, settings.
- **Player** — joins via an invite link. Sees the lore the GM has made
  visible to them, manages their own character(s), and has read-only or
  filtered access to a handful of GM tools (Chronicler chat, Session Log,
  party roster if the GM enabled it).

Every page in this wiki calls out which role(s) it applies to.
